Shawerni Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026 · Publisher: TheNibras Technologies (Dubai, United Arab Emirates)

Shawerni (شاورني) is a free UAE auto-consultant app. It shows information screens about car systems, services, and brands, and offers a Call Now button that opens WhatsApp so you can message our consultant. This page explains how the app handles privacy.

What the app does not collect

Shawerni does not collect, store, or share personal data. There are:

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No backend servers of ours that receive your information

WhatsApp contact

When you tap Call Now, the app opens WhatsApp (or a WhatsApp web page) with a pre-filled message to +971 50 404 9943. That conversation is handled by WhatsApp / Meta under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy. TheNibras does not receive your WhatsApp messages through the Shawerni app itself; any messages you send are part of your WhatsApp chat with us.

App Store distribution

Apple (and Google, if you install from Google Play) distribute the app and may process data as described in their own privacy policies. We do not receive your Apple ID or Google account details from those stores for Shawerni.

Children

Shawerni is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
